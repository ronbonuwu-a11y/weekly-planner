# Weekly Planner

Enter everything you need to do each week, and the planner builds your schedule.

**Open it:** https://ronbonuwu-a11y.github.io/weekly-planner/

## What it does
- **Fixed-time events** (school, practice, dinner) are locked in, and everything else is planned around them.
- **Daily chores** (walking the dog twice a day) are spread across the day.
- **Flexible tasks** (gym 3× a week) go on the days with the most free time, avoiding back-to-back days.
- **Priorities** decide what gets bumped when the week is too full, and anything that doesn't fit is listed.
- Wake-up time, bedtime and a break between tasks are respected.

Your tasks are saved in your browser. Use **Save to file / Load from file** to back them up or move them between devices.
